Contact

Questions, bug reports, feature ideas, or anything about your data — here's how to reach the project. You're welcome to use whichever suits you.

Email

For private questions (e.g. about your account or data), email the maintainer directly. The address is assembled in your browser, so it isn't sitting in the page source for spam bots to harvest.

GitHub

For bugs, feature requests, and public questions, open an issue on GitHub. This is the best place for anything others might benefit from seeing (it's public and searchable). A GitHub account is required.
