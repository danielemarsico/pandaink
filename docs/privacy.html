Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 July 2026

PandaInk is a free, open-source app that syncs drawings from Wacom Bamboo SmartPads (Spark, Slate, Folio) over Bluetooth LE and lets you keep them locally or in a cloud location you control. This policy explains what data the app touches, why, and how you can remove it. It covers the web app at danielemarsico.github.io/pandaink; the desktop app stores everything locally on your own PC and sends nothing to us.

The short version

  • Your drawings are stored on your device (in the browser) and, optionally, in a cloud location you own and choose — your Google Drive, your Dropbox, or the app's Supabase storage.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your drawings for any purpose other than showing them back to you.
  • Bluetooth communication with your tablet happens entirely in your browser. Pen data is not sent to our servers (the one exception — opt-in live sharing — is described below).
  • You can delete any drawing, disconnect any provider, or delete your whole account at any time.

What we collect and why

Account information

When you create an account we use Supabase (our authentication and database provider) to store your email address and a display name. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, we receive your email and basic profile identifier from that provider to create your account. We use this only to sign you in and associate your data with you.

Device registration

To reconnect to your tablet, we store the pairing identifier your tablet generates (a 6-byte device UUID), the protocol type, and a device name you provide. This lets the app talk to your specific SmartPad; it is not personally identifying on its own.

Your drawings

Drawings are pen-stroke coordinates, timestamps, and tablet dimensions. They are always saved locally in your browser (IndexedDB). If you connect a cloud provider, each drawing is also copied there:

  • Google Drive — stored in the hidden, app-private appDataFolder of your own Drive. We can only see files this app created; we cannot see the rest of your Drive.
  • Dropbox — stored in this app's dedicated app folder inside your own Dropbox.
  • Supabase Storage — stored in a private, per-user folder on the app's Supabase project (the free tier, limited to 10 drawings).

Cloud access tokens

When you connect Google Drive or Dropbox, we store the OAuth access/refresh tokens (in Supabase, tied to your account) so the app can upload and read your drawings without asking you to sign in every time. You can revoke these at any time by disconnecting the provider in the app, or from your Google/Dropbox account settings.

Payments (Pro upgrade)

The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Ko-fi. We never see or store your card details. Ko-fi notifies our backend of a completed payment (including the payer email) so we can mark your account as Pro. Please use your PandaInk account email when purchasing so the upgrade can be matched to your account.

Google user data — Limited Use disclosure

PandaInk requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, which grants access only to a hidden application-data folder that this app creates in your Google Drive. It cannot read, modify, or delete any other file in your Drive.

PandaInk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically, data obtained via Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the app's drawing-storage feature for you, is not transferred to others except as necessary to provide that feature or as required by law, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans except with your explicit consent, for security or to comply with applicable law.

Bluetooth and live sharing

Connecting to your tablet uses the Web Bluetooth API, which runs entirely in your browser. Pen data read from the tablet is processed locally and is not transmitted to our servers as part of normal syncing.

If you explicitly turn on live sharing, the pen strokes of that session are relayed in real time through our Cloudflare backend to viewers you share the link with. This relay holds the strokes only for the moment they are forwarded and does not store them. Live sharing is off unless you enable it.

Third-party services

These providers process data on our behalf or at your direction. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and free-tier storage.
  • Google — sign-in and Google Drive storage (at your choice).
  • Dropbox — cloud storage (at your choice).
  • GitHub — sign-in (at your choice) and site hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Cloudflare — backend for secure token exchange and live sharing.
  • Ko-fi — payments for the Pro upgrade and donations.

Data retention and deletion

  • Delete a drawing — removes it from your device and, if connected, from your cloud provider.
  • Disconnect a provider — removes the stored access tokens for it.
  • Forget a device — removes the stored pairing information.
  • Delete your account — permanently removes your profile, device registration, and stored cloud tokens. Drawings kept in your own Google Drive or Dropbox remain under your control and can be deleted from those services directly.

Children

PandaInk is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date; the history is public in the project's Git repository.
